READING
  1. The recovery series decreases across windows in every gradient
     condition AND in the labeled oracle, which minimizes
     cross-entropy on ground-truth labels and shares no mechanism
     with entropy minimization. A trend common to supervised and
     label-free adaptation is a property of the windows, not of the
     adaptation signal.
  2. The frozen baseline RISES across the same three windows. Recovery
     is a difference against that rising baseline, so a falling
     recovery and a flat adapted accuracy are the same observation.
  3. Adapted absolute accuracy is NOT monotone in any condition:
     window 3 is the best window for every method. There is no
     downward trend in performance to explain.
  4. Normalized by each window's own gap, the trend disappears in
     every condition except unfiltered adaptation, whose damage is
     expected to scale with the share of high-entropy drifted flows.
  5. Consistency with the depth trace: the three report windows span
     test batches 0 to 599 only, and their baseline spread (1.71 p) is
     smaller than the trendless scatter of the W-47 depth probe
     (range 4.2 p). Three windows drawn from one early stretch of the
     week are not evidence of a week-long trend, and a monotone
     ordering of three exchangeable values occurs by chance one time
     in six.
